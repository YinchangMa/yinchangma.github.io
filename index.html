Ferroics, Spins & Devices

Two-dimensional materials · Interfacial physics · Nanoelectronics

Hello, I’m Yinchang.

Postdoctoral Fellow · King Abdullah University of Science and Technology

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at KAUST, studying functional two-dimensional materials and the physics that connects them to nanoelectronic devices.

My research interests span ferroelectrics and antiferroelectrics, van der Waals magnetism, and spintronics. I am interested in how polarization, magnetic order, and interfaces can be used to control electronic behavior.

My publications include work in Nature Communications, Nano Letters, and Materials Horizons.

Research interests

Exploring polarization and spin in layered materials, from fundamental mechanisms to functional devices.

01 / Polar order

Ferroelectrics & antiferroelectrics

Switchable polarization, phase transitions, and resistance switching in layered ferroic materials.

02 / Spin & interfaces

van der Waals magnetism

Magnetic order and interfacial proximity effects in low-dimensional systems, with connections to spintronics.

03 / Materials to devices

Nanoelectronic devices

Van der Waals heterostructures, nonvolatile memories, and ferroelectric memristor architectures for neuromorphic computing.
