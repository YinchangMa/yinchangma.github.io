Research expertise

Materials design & synthesis

AI-assisted density functional theory and high-throughput screening; chemical vapor transport and chemical vapor deposition; composition and defect engineering.

Nanofabrication & integration

L-edit and AutoCAD layout design; electron-beam lithography, laser direct writing, photolithography, sputtering, and reactive-ion etching; van der Waals heterostructure integration.

Electrical & magnetic measurements

Temperature-dependent electrical transport, Hall effect, magnetoresistance, tunneling magnetoresistance, and quantum oscillations; PPMS and SQUID measurements.

Structural & functional characterization

Second-harmonic generation, Raman spectroscopy, SEM, TEM, and piezoresponse force microscopy; semiconductor carrier and mobility analysis.

Teaching & mentoring

Guest lecturer for graduate courses Electronic Properties of Materials and Crystallography and Diffraction at KAUST. Contributions include English-language teaching, laboratory guidance, examination preparation, and student support.

Mentoring Ph.D., M.S., and visiting students in experimental design and data analysis; coordinating international collaborations and contributing to research proposal development.

Invited oral presentations at Zhejiang University, UESTC, Nanchang University, and Oxford Instruments forums.
